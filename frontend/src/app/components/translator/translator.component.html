What is Corporate Diplomat?

Corporate Diplomat is a free online translator that converts casual, everyday language into polished corporate jargon — and decodes business buzzwords back into plain English. Whether you need to sound more professional in an email, a Slack message, or a board update — or you just received a message full of phrases like circle back, move the needle, and take this offline and want to know what it actually means — Corporate Diplomat does the work for you in seconds.

Features

  • Casual → Corporate: rewrites plain English into business jargon and professional phrasing.
  • Corporate → Casual: decodes MBA-speak and meeting-speak into everyday language.
  • Curated glossary: 80+ common corporate terms (synergy, bandwidth, deep dive, leverage, drill down, operationalize, value-add, deliverable, RACI, SLA, SWOT, elevator pitch) keep translations consistent.
  • AI style review: a second pass verifies tone and clarity before returning the final output.
  • One-click swap & copy: move output back to input, flip direction, and copy with a click.

Who it is for

Corporate Diplomat is built for new employees onboarding into corporate culture, cross-functional teams collaborating across departments, founders writing investor updates, job seekers polishing cover letters, international professionals navigating English business jargon, and anyone who has ever stared at an email full of low-hanging fruit and boil-the-ocean warnings and wondered what it really meant.
