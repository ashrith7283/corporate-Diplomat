Corporate Diplomat — Casual to Corporate & Corporate to Casual Translator

Corporate Diplomat is the free online tool that translates between everyday casual language and polished corporate jargon. Whether you need to sound more professional in a Slack message, an email to leadership, or an investor update — or you just received an email full of buzzwords like circle back, move the needle, and take this offline and want to know what it actually means — Corporate Diplomat does the work for you in seconds.

What Corporate Diplomat does

  • Casual → Corporate: rewrites plain English into business jargon, professional tone, and corporate-ready phrasing.
  • Corporate → Casual: decodes corporate buzzwords, MBA-speak, and meeting-speak into everyday language.
  • Built-in glossary: a curated dictionary of 80+ common corporate terms (synergy, bandwidth, deep dive, leverage, drill down, operationalize, value-add, deliverable, RACI, SLA, SWOT, elevator pitch, etc.) keeps translations accurate and consistent.
  • AI-powered style review: a second agent verifies tone, clarity, and intent before the final output is returned.
  • One-click swap & copy: move the output back into the input, flip direction, and copy the result to your clipboard.

Who it is for

Corporate Diplomat is built for new employees onboarding into corporate culture, cross-functional teams collaborating across departments, founders and operators writing investor or board updates, job seekers polishing cover letters, international professionals navigating English business jargon, and anyone who has ever stared at an email full of low-hanging fruit and boil-the-ocean warnings and wondered what it really meant.

Frequently asked questions

What is Corporate Diplomat?

Corporate Diplomat is a free online translator that converts casual language into corporate jargon and decodes corporate speak into plain, everyday English. It is powered by AI agents and a curated glossary of more than 80 common corporate terms.

How do I use it?

Open the app, pick a direction (Make it Corporate or Make it Casual), paste your text, and press Translate. The output appears in seconds, with notes explaining any tricky terms.

Is it free?

Yes. Corporate Diplomat is free to use in your browser.

What corporate terms does it understand?

The glossary covers 80+ common business phrases such as circle back, low-hanging fruit, move the needle, deep dive, take this offline, drill down, leverage, synergize, operationalize, value-add, deliverable, RACI, SLA, SWOT, elevator pitch, and many more.
